**Red de Intercambio Federada** is a decentralized mutual-credit accounting system (zero-sum "Mano Vuelta" ledger) designed for federated community barter networks.

### Features

- **Zero-sum mutual credit ledger**: No virtual currency is minted out of thin air. Every node manages a symmetric mutual credit ledger (TQ) recording community contributions and commitments where the global sum is always zero.
- **Energy-based pricing model**: Valuation based on embodied energy per kg of material (ICE Database / Agribalyse standard) plus labor hours.
- **Multilateral and bilateral node federation**: Cross-node transfers using Ed25519 signatures, mTLS transport, global multilateral pools, and bilateral agreements.
- **Composite products & community store**: Automatic energy cost calculation for recipes and composite goods, hierarchical assembly-governed catalog, and personal community storefronts.
- **QR & offline ESP32 NFC payments**: Dynamic QR payments and physical offline NFC card terminals with forward secrecy and federated card resolution.
- **Local & federated democratic governance**: General and scoped assemblies, quorum management, community organizations, departments, and public website generator.
